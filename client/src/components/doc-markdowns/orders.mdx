# Order Management

The Orders module provides end-to-end fulfillment tracking for customer orders—from placement to invoice generation and inventory deduction.

---

## Order Fulfillment Workflow

Every order moves through a clear, structured progression:

1. **Pending**
   * The order is placed by the customer (via API, store checkout, or mobile app).
   * A draft invoice is created automatically in the background to reserve pricing and line items.

2. **Processing**
   * Your team accepts and begins preparing the items for packaging.
   * The customer receives an automated confirmation that their order is being prepared.

3. **Dispatched**
   * The order is handed over to the delivery partner or shipping courier.
   * Enter the **Delivery Reference / Tracking ID** and optional dispatch notes.
   * The customer receives an email notification with their tracking details.

4. **Completed**
   * The customer receives the package.
   * The draft invoice is automatically converted to **Issued**.
   * Product inventory is deducted for tracked items, and revenue is recorded.

> [!NOTE]
> **Cancellation & Rejections:** An order can be marked as **`Rejected`** from any active state before completion. When rejected, a cancellation reason is recorded and an email notice is sent to the customer.

---

## Lifecycle States Overview

| Status | Stage | Customer Notification | Inventory & Invoice Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`PENDING`** | Order Placed | Order Received | Draft invoice created; no stock deduction |
| **`PROCESSING`** | In Preparation | Preparation in Progress | Draft invoice retained |
| **`DISPATCHED`** | Shipped | Dispatched with Tracking ID | Shipping reference recorded |
| **`COMPLETED`** | Fulfilled | Receipt & Completion Confirmation | Draft invoice **ISSUED**; inventory stock deducted |
| **`REJECTED`** | Cancelled | Order Cancellation Notice | Order closed with recorded reason |

## Managing Orders in the Dashboard

### 1. Status Filter & Quick Search
* **Status Filter**: Use the selector to view orders by state (`Pending`, `Processing`, `Dispatched`, `Completed`, `Rejected`, or `All Orders`). Defaults to **Pending** for fast access to new orders.
* **Debounced Search**: Instantly find orders by Order Number (e.g. `ORD-XXXX-XXXX`) or customer name and phone number.

### 2. Direct Status Transitions
From the Orders table or the Order Details page, click **Update Status**:
* **Mark as Processing**: Acknowledges the order and triggers a preparation notice.
* **Dispatch Order**: Prompts for the carrier tracking/consignment number (`deliveryReference`) and optional delivery instructions.
* **Complete Order**: Finalizes fulfillment, issues the linked invoice, updates stock balances, and logs revenue.
* **Reject Order**: Cancels the order with a recorded explanation.

### 3. Comprehensive Order Details
Click any **Order Number** link to view the complete order breakdown:
* **Customer Profile**: Contact information, delivery address, and a direct link to the customer directory.
* **Delivery & Tracking**: Consignment number, courier notes, and status timeline.
* **Linked Invoice**: Current invoice status, payment dues, and document preview/print tools.
* **Line Items Table**: Product names, SKUs, quantity, pricing tiers, and line totals.
* **Financial Breakdown**: Subtotal, coupon discounts, applicable taxes, shipping fees, and grand total.
