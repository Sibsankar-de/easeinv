# Access Control & Permissions

Manage team member permissions, invite collaborators, and configure role-based access for your store.

---

## Overview

EaseInv provides granular **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)** allowing store owners to invite teammates, assign specific responsibilities, and protect sensitive financial and store settings.

Key capabilities include:
* **Email Invitations**: Securely invite registered EaseInv users to collaborate on your store.
* **Role Hierarchy**: Tiered roles tailored for store operations (`Owner`, `Manager`, `Employee`).
* **Dynamic Role Updates**: Promote or modify permissions anytime without re-inviting users.
* **Instant Revocation**: Remove store access with immediate session invalidation.

---

## Store Roles & Permission Hierarchy

| Role | Description | Access & Permissions |
|---|---|---|
| **`OWNER`** | Store Creator / Primary Account | Full unrestricted access to store data, billing subscriptions, API integrations, and store deletion. Cannot be removed or modified. |
| **`MANAGER`** | Store Manager / Supervisor | Access to all operational modules (Inventory, Billing, Orders, Customers, Shipping Profiles) and team invitation management. |
| **`EMPLOYEE`** | Staff / Sales Associate | Operational access to create invoices, record customer payments, update order fulfillment states, and view product stock counts. |

> [!NOTE]
> **Owner Protection**: Store ownership is permanently bound to the creator. Owners cannot be demoted or removed by other team members.

## Managing Team Access in the Dashboard

### 1. Navigating to Access Control
1. Open your store dashboard.
2. Go to **Settings → Security & Access** from the left navigation menu.
3. Select the **Access Control** tab.

### 2. Inviting a New Team Member
1. Click the **Invite new user** button (or top navbar **New invite**).
2. Enter the collaborator's registered **Email Address**.
3. Select their initial **Role** (`Manager` or `Employee`).
4. Click **Send invitation**. The user will receive access to collaborate on the store.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The invited user must already have a registered EaseInv account with that email address.

### 3. Searching & Filtering Accessors
Use the real-time debounced search bar at the top of the team table to instantly filter members by name or email.

### 4. Updating a Member's Role
1. In the team table, locate the user you wish to modify.
2. Click the **Edit** (pencil icon) button in the **Actions** column.
3. Choose the updated role from the dropdown (`Manager` or `Employee`).
4. Click **Update Role** to apply the change immediately.

### 5. Revoking Store Access
1. Click the **Delete** (trash icon) button next to the team member.
2. Confirm the action in the prompt. The user's access to all store data will be revoked immediately.
